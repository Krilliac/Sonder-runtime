# WP2 LOOP-007/008 — Retry and idempotency policy

## Scope

`sonder_runtime.domain.loop_retry_policy` is a pure domain policy module. It
does not sleep, call transports, reconcile state, persist idempotency records,
or modify the existing cancellation, event, or repository modules.

## Contract

- Failures are classified as `transient`, `throttled`, `unknown_outcome`, or
  `permanent` from explicit status/code metadata. A missing outcome after
  dispatch is always `unknown_outcome`, including a timeout.
- Retry metadata is bounded: callers provide the attempt limit and receive
  exponential-full-jitter parameters, a cap for the attempt, optional upstream
  `Retry-After`, and an optional caller deadline. The adapter owns sleeping and
  random jitter.
- Retries are bounded by both the attempt limit and the caller's deadline.
  Permanent failures do not retry; throttled failures preserve their upstream
  delay hint.
- Operations without side effects may retry after a retryable failure.
  Idempotent operations must reuse one stable idempotency key. Non-idempotent
  operations require a unique key and reconciliation before replay.
- Any unknown side-effect outcome requires reconciliation before another
  effectful attempt. Reconciliation is a caller/adapter responsibility and
  must establish whether the original effect committed, is still in flight, or
  can safely be replayed.
- The policy describes requirements only; it does not claim exactly-once
  transport. Exactly-once effects require durable deduplication/reconciliation
  at the side-effect boundary.
